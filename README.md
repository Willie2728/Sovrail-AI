# SOVRAIL ONE™
## Autonomous AI Trust, Execution & Recovery Platform

SOVRAIL ONE is Wilkerson Collective Labs' unified enterprise control plane for autonomous AI work. It combines three preserved internal modules:

- **SWARMER Trust & Authority** — determines whether an agent, capability, action, data flow, MCP server, skill, connector, model artifact or delegated business action is trusted and authorized.
- **SOVRAIL Execution Intelligence** — determines how approved work should execute across providers, models, gears, harnesses, caches, local/cloud/substrate routes, regions and resilience paths by optimizing total cost per verified successful outcome.
- **KAMERON Continuity & Recovery** — preserves trustworthy state, validates recovery eligibility, prevents duplicate consequential side effects and resumes interrupted work from an approved checkpoint.

## Core promise
**Trust it. Route it. Prove it. Recover it.**

SOVRAIL ONE does not replace a customer's firewall, EDR/XDR, IAM/PAM, SIEM/SOAR, SASE/CASB, DLP, email security or cloud-security stack. It operates across and above those controls to govern autonomous AI actors whose work crosses multiple enterprise surfaces.

## The control loop
`Intent → Identity → Capability Trust → Delegated Authority → Route Selection → Execution → External Outcome Proof → Checkpoint → Recovery/Containment`

## Defensible architecture
- **Capability supply-chain trust and continuous revalidation**
- **Per-action delegated business authority**
- **Bidirectional data trust and provenance**
- **Cross-agent communication/collusion and unauthorized external-memory detection**
- **Stateless MCP per-request assurance and state-handle integrity**
- **Runtime exposure and backend-authority verification**
- **Blast-radius isolation and short-lived scoped credentials**
- **Machine-speed containment/circuit breaking**
- **SOVRAIL Verified Outcome Economics** — optimize successful business outcomes, not cheapest token price
- **Adaptive Recipe Memory** — reuse validated portions of recurring workflows without blindly reusing stale output
- **GearShift + Provider/Surface/Harness/Substrate Sparring** — controlled evidence-based comparison across model effort levels and execution paths
- **Failure-Domain Independence** — backups are scored for correlated infrastructure dependencies, not just provider name
- **Outcome Integrity / Outcome Proof** — verify consequential external results rather than trusting an agent's completion claim
- **KAMERON exactly-once consequence protection** — safe recovery without duplicate emails, payments, contracts or other irreversible side effects
- **Execution Provenance Ledger** — reconstruct the actual agent/model/tool/policy/credential/checkpoint path that produced an outcome

## Launch scope
The investor/design-partner release is deliberately frozen around five proofs:
1. SWARMER blocks an unauthorized consequential action.
2. SOVRAIL compares approved execution routes and chooses using measured outcome economics.
3. Adaptive Recipe Memory reduces avoidable recomputation.
4. KAMERON resumes after failure without repeating a committed side effect.
5. Outcome Proof independently verifies the external business result and produces an evidence record.

Everything else is supporting infrastructure or later roadmap unless required to make these proofs safe and real.

## Overlay, not rip-and-replace
SOVRAIL ONE is designed to work with existing enterprise products and gateways, including EDR/XDR, IAM/PAM, SIEM/SOAR, cloud controls, email/collaboration systems and AI gateways. A customer may retain Palo Alto, CrowdStrike, Microsoft, Okta, CyberArk, Splunk, Kong, Boomi, F5, Cloudflare, Portkey/Prisma AIRS, LiteLLM or other infrastructure while SOVRAIL ONE adds agent-aware authority, execution intelligence, verified outcomes and trusted recovery above them.

## Standalone module preservation
SWARMER, SOVRAIL Execution Intelligence and KAMERON remain named modules with separable interfaces so they can still be licensed, embedded, OEM'd or strategically transacted independently if needed. The flagship commercial packaging is **SOVRAIL ONE**.
